Unify gives you a fully remote virtual employee — an AI assistant you can talk to
the way you'd talk to any other colleague. Message it, email it, call it, or hop
on a video call and share your screen. It remembers your conversations, works on
tasks in the background, and reaches out when it has something for you.

These docs explain everything your assistant can do, and how to get the most out
of working together.
