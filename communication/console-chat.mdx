The chat panel in the [Console](https://console.unify.ai) is the fastest way to
talk to your assistant — no setup, no phone numbers, no connected accounts.
Open your assistant's page and start typing.

## What you can do

- **Send messages** — just type into the composer, exactly like any messaging
  app.
- **Attach files** — drop documents, spreadsheets, images, or anything else
  into the chat. Your assistant reads attachments and can work with them
  directly.
- **Speak instead of type** — use the microphone button to record a message.
  Your speech is transcribed into the composer, so you can review it before
  sending.
- **React with emoji** — react to your assistant's messages the way you would
  in any chat app. It notices your reactions.
- **Hear replies out loud** — if your assistant has a voice configured, its
  chat messages can be spoken aloud.

## It's always on

Console chat is your assistant's home channel. Even while you're mid-call, or
waiting for it to email you something, the chat thread stays live — your
assistant posts updates, links, and deliverables there so you always have a
written record.

When your assistant is working on something long-running, it replies in chat as
soon as there's something worth telling you, rather than making you watch a
spinner.

## Spending limits

If your assistant has a monthly spending cap and it's been reached, the chat
composer tells you so and pauses until the cap resets or is raised. You can
adjust spending caps from the assistant's profile.

<Tip>
  Chat is the best place to ask your assistant *about* the platform itself.
  Questions like "how do I set up your email?" get an answer plus an offer to
  walk you through it on a screen-share call.
</Tip>
