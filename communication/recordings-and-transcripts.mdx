Your assistant keeps a faithful record of its communication — that's part of
what makes it useful. Nothing you discussed is ever "lost to the phone call".

## Transcripts

Every conversation — chat messages, emails, texts, WhatsApp messages, and
spoken calls — is transcribed into your assistant's conversation history.
That's what lets you switch channels mid-topic and have your assistant follow
along seamlessly.

## Call recordings

Voice calls — [Unify Meet](/communication/unify-meet),
[phone](/communication/phone-calls), and
[WhatsApp calls](/communication/whatsapp) — are recorded automatically. When
the recording is ready, a link is attached to the conversation, so you can
listen back to exactly what was said.

## Where to find things

The Console is your window into all of it: open your assistant's page to
review conversation history, and look through its activity to see what was
sent, received, and said on your behalf.
